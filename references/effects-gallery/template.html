sfv-edit effects

Every effect you can write in plan.md, looping on a stand-in speaker. Overlays use the deilbach look (teal bar, orange accent word). Motion is slowed and exaggerated so you can see it on a loop. Each card shows the plan line that triggers the effect.

Sound sfx · click to play

The files in assets/sfx. Use - sfx whoosh-short @word vol=0.6. Material effects play a whoosh and punch words play a pop on their own (style sfx map, gain 0.75). In deilbach, cutaways have no sound effect.

Any other sound: generate it with ElevenLabs (the sound-effects skill), e.g. "river rushing past" or "rain on a window, 2 s". Save the mp3 into assets/sfx and use it by name: - sfx river-rush @water. Your ElevenLabs key is in videos/.env.local. The free plan is for non-commercial use only.
